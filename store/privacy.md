# Translitra Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-16

Translitra is a browser extension that transliterates text between Cyrillic and Latin alphabets.

## What the extension processes

- The text you select on a web page, type into the extension's popup or converter window, or pass through the context menu or keyboard shortcut.

Access to web page content is optional. It is requested through Chrome's permission prompt only when you turn on the selection tooltip, and it is released again when you turn the tooltip off.

All processing happens locally inside your browser. The extension makes no network requests.

## What the extension stores

- Your settings (transliteration standard, tooltip options, theme) in Chrome's extension storage, which may be synced by Chrome across your devices if you are signed in.
- The size and position of the converter window, locally.

Selected or converted text is never stored and never leaves your device.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.
- It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
- It does not load remote code.
